Who We Are

Built by students.
Proven in the dirt.

Galactus Racing is the official Baja team of Rajarambapu Institute of Technology. Since 2008, our engineers have designed, fabricated and raced single-seat off-road vehicles that compete against the best college teams in India.

Our mission: turn classroom engineering into machines that survive rocks, mud and four-hour endurance races, and turn students into engineers industry wants to hire.

What is Baja SAE?

A student engineering competition where teams build a rugged off-road car around a standard engine. Cars are judged on design, cost and sales presentations, then pushed through acceleration, hill climb, suspension and a punishing endurance race.
