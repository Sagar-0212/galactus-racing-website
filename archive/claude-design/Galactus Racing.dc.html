Who we are RIT Rajaramnagar · Since 2008

Galactus Racing is the official Baja team of Rajarambapu Institute of Technology. Since 2008 we have designed, fabricated and raced single-seat off-road cars against the best college teams in India.

What is Baja SAE? The format

Teams build a rugged off-road car around a standard engine. Cars are judged on design, cost and sales, then pushed through acceleration, hill climb, suspension and a four-hour endurance race.
